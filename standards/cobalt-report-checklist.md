# Cobalt.io — How to Write a Great Vulnerability Report

Source: `cobalt-report-guide.md` in [`references/`](../references/)

## Core sections Cobalt.io recommends

1. **Explicit title** — name the vulnerability class and target.
2. **Short referenced description** — no Wikipedia or poor-quality sources.
3. **Step-by-step proof of concept** — treat the triager as a newcomer.
4. **Criticality assessment** — confidentiality, integrity, availability, plus business impact.
5. **Tools used** — list scanners, browsers, scripts, etc.
6. **Attachments** — screenshots, video, audio, terminal output.
7. **Suggested fixes/solutions** — concrete examples, not just "sanitize input".

## Anti-patterns from Cobalt.io

- Do not copy-paste automated-tool output.
- Do not cite Wikipedia or low-credibility sites.
- Do not assume the triager knows the context; spell out every step.

## Why I use it

Cobalt.io's guidance keeps reports actionable and triager-friendly, which shortens response time and reduces back-and-forth.
